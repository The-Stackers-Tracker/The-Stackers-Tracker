Phone Lock Test

This page checks if your phone's screen lock can protect The Stackers Tracker. It doesn't touch the app or your data.

Step 1: tap Set Up Test. Your phone asks for your screen lock and may offer to save a passkey. Say yes.

Step 2: tap Run Test and unlock again.

Results show here.

When done, send a screenshot of the result. You can delete the "Lock Test" passkey from your phone's password settings afterwards.
