# The Stackers Tracker

Track your gold, silver and other precious metals: what you paid, what it's worth today, and what you're still hunting for.

**Open the app: https://the-stackers-tracker.github.io/The-Stackers-Tracker/**

- Live spot prices for silver, gold, platinum, palladium, rhodium and copper, in your own currency
- Bullion, junk silver and collectible coins, with photos, places and sales
- Goals, date sets and collector values
- No account and no sign-up. Your data stays on your own device.

## Install it

The app runs in your browser. Installing it gives it its own icon and lets it work without internet. It's free and optional. The app shows these steps the first time you open it, and they're also under **Settings → Help & about → Installing the app**.

### Android (Chrome, Brave or Samsung Internet)

1. Open the link above.
2. Tap the browser's menu: **⋮** at the top or bottom (**≡** in Samsung Internet).
3. Tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**, then **Install**.
4. Open The Stackers Tracker from its new icon.

### iPhone or iPad

1. Open the link above in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button: the square with an arrow pointing up (bottom of the screen, or top right on iPad).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Open The Stackers Tracker from its new icon, and set it up there.

Install it **before** you add anything. On iPhone and iPad the home-screen app keeps its own data, separate from Safari.

### Computer (Windows or Mac)

1. Open the link above in **Chrome** or **Edge**.
2. Click the **install icon** at the right end of the address bar, or open the browser menu and choose **Install The Stackers Tracker**.
3. Click **Install**.

On a Mac in Safari, choose **File → Add to Dock**. Firefox can't install apps, but the site works fine in a normal tab.

## Your data

Everything you enter stays in your browser on that device. Nothing is sent to us. Clearing your browser data or losing your phone erases it, so export a backup now and then (**Settings → Backup → Export**) and keep the file somewhere safe. To move to a new device, open the app there and import that file.

Live prices and collector values need free keys from [MetalpriceAPI](https://metalpriceapi.com) and [Numista](https://en.numista.com/api/). The app walks you through this. You can also type prices in by hand.

## What's new
